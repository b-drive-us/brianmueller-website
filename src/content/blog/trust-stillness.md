For decades I kept moving. I was too afraid to slow down, certain that if I ever stopped
I would slip below the waves.

It turns out that is not what happens.

*Trust Stillness* is a new collection of poems, and it comes from a place I could not have
written from twenty years ago — the place you reach when you finally become content to drift,
and let the waves carry you. That is when you may discover you can breathe under water.

These are poems for anyone learning, slowly, to surrender into the second half of life.

Here is one of them.

<div class="poem poem-left">When getting a physical,
the doctor may test
the strength of our grip
to measure aging and health.
Yet whether we are young or old,
fit or beginning to fail,
there's something good happening
when we slowly release
all the fear and uncertainty
we've been desperately clutching
and clinging to for survival.
As our ability to break rocks
and hurl stones diminishes,
we may begin to feel something new,
something softer emerging
in the palms of our hands.
We grip and grind our way through life
until grace arrives asking us
to let go.</div>
